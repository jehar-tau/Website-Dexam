# Learner report requirements

Owner direction: 1 November 2026. Report presentation version: `discovery-report-v2`.

The report is a personal practice guide. Lead with specific development needs and useful next actions. Keep the approved Discovery visual language.

- Show up to three practice priorities, selected from submitted work. Describe the less consistent subskills within an area, so a low area average does not label every related ability as weak.
- Explain what to strengthen, why it matters, what to study and one small experiment to try. Suggestions based on a single activity must be provisional. Strong attempts receive stretch directions rather than invented deficits.
- Make everyday observation concrete: signs, menus, packaging, shelves, spaces and interactions. Include a question to ask and something to record or remake.
- Provide a ten-minute daily observation/making/reflection loop, and a four-week plan with a small weekly output and an observable way to review progress.
- Keep skill-area scores in a compact overview. Do not display a question-by-question score list, response gallery, rubric audit or links back to individual marked answers in the learner report or its exported copy. Internal scoring evidence remains available to the engine for consistency and future calibration.
- Offer a direct PDF download sized for A4 paper, with at least three substantive pages. The current implementation has five pages: priorities, everyday habits, weeks 1-2, weeks 3-4 and a compact skill overview with learning resources. Keep readable print typography, embedded fonts, page numbers, consistent margins and complete content without clipping or split cards. Verify every rendered page after layout changes.
- Keep scoring methodology and limitations in a small optional section. Do not make implementation details the main content.
- Use first-party educational resources as optional further study. The original practice prompts are suggestions, not claims of a validated intervention or guaranteed improvement.
- Preserve scores, saved answers and the current payment boundary. No AI call, percentile, confidence/personality inference or career suitability verdict is introduced.
- Keep personal attempts and report copies outside the public repository. The current report remains a development preview until publication and secure paid delivery are separately ready.

Validation includes unchanged scoring, incomplete-attempt handling, deterministic guidance, desktop/mobile accessibility, saved-answer preservation, weekly plan controls and a complete coaching PDF without answer-level marks. Check the PDF's A4 media boxes, five-page count, embedded fonts, scores including 100, local download and font-fetch failure/retry behaviour.
