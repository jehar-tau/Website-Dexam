# DEXAM competitive and AI-search baseline

Recorded: 1 November 2026, Asia/Kolkata

Task: T05

Public evidence: `growth/SEARCH_VISIBILITY_BASELINE.csv` and `growth/AI_SEARCH_PROMPTS.csv`

## Method

- Fourteen fixed Google Search queries were sampled in signed-in Chrome with `hl=en`, `gl=in` and `pws=0`.
- Google displayed a Pune device-location context. The precise device-derived locality is intentionally withheld from the repository.
- The browser used its desktop viewport. This is not a mobile-rank sample.
- Twelve samples displayed Google’s “Results are not personalised” label. Two did not; their rows are marked rather than assumed non-personalised.
- “First eight web results” means the first eight visible ordinary result headings captured from page one. Sponsored placements, the signed-in account’s private Ads summary, AI responses and local-pack cards were excluded.
- Local-pack presence records only the visible three-place sample. It does not claim an absolute map rank.
- Five fixed Google AI Mode prompts were sampled separately. A mention, direct website link and source citation are different evidence and are labelled separately.

Search results vary by time, device, location, language, account state and Google experiments. This baseline is a dated observation, not a guaranteed rank. It must be repeated with the same prompts and method before making trend claims.

## Findings

### Local discovery is the strongest current visibility

DEXAM appeared in six of the eight local-intent samples’ visible map packs:

- `NID coaching Pune`
- `UCEED coaching Pune`
- `NIFT entrance coaching Pune`
- `NATA coaching Pune`
- `design coaching Hadapsar`
- `design coaching Amanora Pune`

DEXAM did not appear in the sampled three-place pack for the broader `design entrance coaching Pune` query. The `design portfolio guidance Pune` result set did not expose a comparable coaching map pack in the captured sample.

This supports maintaining accurate Google Business Profile facts and real classroom/location evidence. It does not justify invented reviews, result counts or ranking claims.

### Ordinary web visibility is the main gap

DEXAM did not appear in the sampled first eight ordinary web results for any of the fourteen queries. The recurring domains were:

- coaching brands with dedicated Pune/exam pages: SILICA, Rathore Design, Ignite India, MAD School, DQ Labs, My Design Academia and iDoodle;
- resource publishers: SelfStudys, Careers360, Toprankers, Roughworks, Testbook and Scribd;
- official exam sources: IIT Bombay UCEED/CEED pages;
- local aggregators: Justdial, UrbanPro and Sulekha.

The gap is page specificity, evidence and discoverability—not a reason to manufacture dozens of thin keyword variants.

### Resource queries reward dedicated useful pages

Paper and mock-test result sets consistently used exam-specific URLs with clear resource intent. Official IIT Bombay pages were prominent for CEED and UCEED papers. Mock-test results emphasized free access, current exam year, instant scoring, All India rank, scheduled open mocks, detailed explanations or drawing feedback.

DEXAM should compete with original, faculty-reviewed practice and transparent scoring/review boundaries. It must not imply that subjective drawings are objectively auto-scored or copy competitor questions.

### AI visibility is currently hyperlocal, not topical

In the five-prompt Google AI Mode sample, DEXAM appeared once: the Amanora Park Town coaching prompt. It appeared as a local business entity with a direct `designexam.com` website link. It was absent from the sampled answers about UCEED mock practice, NID drawing preparation, portfolio guidance and All India NID mocks.

The first improvement target is therefore not “AI optimization” as a separate trick. It is publishable evidence that search and answer systems can cite:

1. exam-specific original practice;
2. official-source-linked paper guidance;
3. a truthful portfolio/studio-feedback method;
4. clear Pune/Amanora location and classroom information;
5. named faculty review only after bios and permissions are approved.
