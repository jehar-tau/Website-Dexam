# DEXAM student feedback and public-proof playbook

Updated: 1 November 2026  
Scope: first two current students preparing for the 2027 cycle

## Purpose

Learn what is working, fix what is not, and—with separate permission—publish genuine student experience. This is not a request for praise. Participation is optional, has no effect on classes, and carries no reward. Do not store names, contact details, private answers or identifiable work in the public task ledger or progress dashboard.

## Sequence

1. Confirm the exam(s) each student is preparing for and whether each is under 18.
2. Invite each student to a separate 10-minute private feedback conversation.
3. Record themes privately and action at least one reasonable improvement.
4. If a student is comfortable, ask separately for one public action: a Google review, an Instagram Story tag, or approval of a specific website testimonial.
5. If the student is under 18, obtain parent/guardian approval for any identifiable quote, image, video, work sample or reshare.
6. Show the exact quote/media, attribution, channel and intended duration before publication.
7. Track only aggregate counts in the dashboard: eligible, private requests, private responses, public reviews and story tags.

## Message 1 — private feedback invitation

> Hi [Name], I’m reviewing how DEXAM’s 2027 preparation is working for you. Could we take 10 minutes for honest feedback? Please tell me what has genuinely helped, what remains confusing, and one thing we should improve. This is private unless we separately ask for your permission to use a specific quote, photo or clip. There is no need for the feedback to be positive.

Ask these questions without leading the answer:

1. Which exam or exams are you preparing for in 2027?
2. What felt most difficult before you joined DEXAM?
3. Which specific class, exercise or feedback has helped so far?
4. Can you show one example of a drawing, decision or habit that improved?
5. What still feels difficult or unclear?
6. If we changed one thing in the next month, what should it be?
7. Would you recommend this learning format to another student? Why or why not?

Do not turn a critical answer into a public quote without resolving the issue and asking separately. Do not ask the student to repeat marketing language.

## Message 2 — neutral Google review request

Send only at the same appropriate milestone to all eligible students/parents. Replace the placeholder with the official Google Business Profile review link; never invent it.

> Hi [Name], if you are comfortable, you may leave an honest Google review about your own experience at DEXAM. Please use your own words and include anything positive or critical that would help another student or parent decide. There is no reward, and whether you review us will not affect your classes. [OFFICIAL GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

Never offer a discount, gift, priority, marks, class benefit or any other incentive. Never suggest a star rating or screen out people who might be critical.

## Message 3 — parent/guardian public-use permission

> Hello [Parent/Guardian], we are documenting the 2027 learning journey at DEXAM. May we invite [Student] to share optional feedback and, only if you both agree, use a specific quote, photo or video on DEXAM’s website or Instagram? Participation is voluntary, has no effect on classes, and you may approve the exact item before publication. We will not publish full names, scores or personal details without separate permission. Please reply with what you approve: private feedback only / quote / photo / video / Instagram Story reshare.

A broad “yes” is not enough for future items. Retain private evidence of the exact asset, channel, attribution choice, approval date and withdrawal contact.

## Message 4 — optional Instagram Story

> Hi [Name], if you would like, you can share one genuine moment from class or your own practice in an Instagram Story and tag @dexamacademy. Please write it in your own words—“today’s practice”, “one thing I understood”, or “working toward [exam] 2027” is enough. This is completely optional. Please don’t show another student or their work unless they have agreed.

Before DEXAM reshares, confirm the student and parent/guardian (when required) approve that specific Story being reshared. Do not require an endorsement, link, hashtag bundle or positive wording.

## Message 5 — exact website-testimonial approval

Draft only from the student's own recorded words, without changing meaning:

> Thank you for the feedback. We would like to use the exact quote below on [website page / Instagram post]. Please check that it accurately reflects your experience. You can edit it, decline it, choose first name / initial / anonymous attribution, and ask us to remove it later. Approving this quote is separate from your classes. Quote: “[EXACT QUOTE]”. Attribution: [CHOICE]. Channel: [CHANNEL].

If under 18, send the same exact item to the parent/guardian and obtain both approvals before publication.

## Quora and Reddit rule

Do not ask the two students to place reviews, mentions or links on Quora or Reddit. Do not give them endorsement copy, coordinate votes, or ask both to post in the same thread.

DEXAM faculty may answer a real Quora question completely and disclose:

> Disclosure: I teach at DEXAM in Pune. The advice below is based on my experience preparing students for design entrance examinations.

If a current student independently chooses to answer a genuinely relevant question and the community allows it, the minimum disclosure is:

> Disclosure: I’m currently a DEXAM student preparing for 2027; this is only my own experience.

The answer must be the student's own words. Add no link unless it is necessary and expressly allowed. For Reddit, check the exact community rules on the day of posting; seek moderator approval before any branded or promotional contribution in `r/uceedtakers`.

## Owner checklist before any public use

- [ ] Student gave candid private feedback without pressure.
- [ ] Exact exam and 2027 context are accurate.
- [ ] Parent/guardian approval exists if required.
- [ ] Exact quote/photo/video/work and channel were approved.
- [ ] Other students, private work, phone numbers and location metadata are absent or separately cleared.
- [ ] No incentive, requested star rating, scripted praise or unverified outcome claim.
- [ ] Withdrawal/removal route is known.
- [ ] Dashboard contains only aggregate counts.
