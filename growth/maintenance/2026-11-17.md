# Seasonal official-source review — 17 November 2026

Scope: date statements on the public 2027 exam overview and planning calendar. Checked official exam/authority pages on 17 November 2026. This is a source audit, not a live feed; the page still needs its normal pull-request review and deployment verification.

## Verified changes

- **NID DAT:** The [NID 2027–28 admission page](https://admissions.nid.edu/NIDA2027/Default.aspx) lists 30 November 2026 as the application deadline and 20 December 2026 for DAT Prelims. DAT Mains timing is still to be announced. The public copy now avoids the prior unsupported March–April estimate.
- **UCEED 2027:** The [official IIT Bombay site](https://www.uceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/) lists the extended regular-fee deadline as 5 November, late-fee registration through 10 November at 5 pm, the exam on 17 January 2027 and results on 6 March 2027. Registration is closed as of this review.
- **CEED 2027:** The [official IIT Bombay site](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/) lists the same registration extensions and 17 January 2027 exam date, with results scheduled for 3 March 2027. Registration is closed as of this review.
- **NIFTEE 2027:** The [NTA NIFTEE page](https://exams.nta.nic.in/niftee/) links a 14 November public notice extending applications without late fee through 24 November, late-fee applications through 29 November, and correction dates to 30 November–2 December. That notice does not state an exam date, so the page no longer predicts a February exam or April situation test.
- **NATA:** The [official schedule page](https://www.nata.in/schedule.html) still displays the 2026 schedule. No 2027 application or exam dates were present there during this review; the 2026 dates are not projected into the 2027 calendar.

## Changes prepared

The exam overview and planning calendar now use the official dates above, mark unannounced dates plainly, and link directly to each official authority. The July 2026 expected-date disclaimer is removed. Eligibility, fees, exam patterns and syllabus statements were outside this date-only review and were not changed.

## Release status

These edits are prepared in a review branch. They are not live until the public website PR passes its checks, receives the required review, is merged and its deployment is verified. Recheck official notices at the next weekly review and sooner if an authority posts an amendment.
